Paper 1: ALL-OPTICAL DISSIPATIVE DISCRETE TIME CRYSTALS
Source: Taheri, Matsko, Maleki, Sacha. Nature Communications 13, 848 (2022)
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-28462-x
PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8844012/

PLATFORM: MgF_2 whispering-gallery-mode ring resonator
  - Cavity radius: ~1.06 mm
  - FSR: 32.8 GHz
  - Loaded resonance bandwidth: 2*pi * 200 kHz
  - GVD (beta_2): ~-4.9 ps^2/km
  - Dispersion coefficient (D_2): 2*pi * 6.8 kHz
  - Pump wavelength: ~1545 nm
  - Pump power entering resonator: ~3 mW

DRIVING PROTOCOL: Two CW pump lasers self-injection-locked to different cavity modes
  - Pump separations demonstrated: M = 3, 4, 5, 8 FSRs
  - Temporal lattice periodicity: T_lattice = T_R / M
  - Round-trip time: T_R = 1/32.8 GHz ~ 30.5 ns

PERIOD MULTIPLICATION OBSERVED:
  - m = 2 (period-doubling) — soliton in every 2nd lattice well
  - m = 3 (period-tripling)
  - m = 4 (period-quadrupling)
  - m = 5 (period-quintupling)
  - m = 8

CUFT-RASP OVERLAP ANALYSIS:
  - Period-5 DTC OBSERVED (M=5 pump separation) [RELEVANT]
  - Period-3 DTC OBSERVED (M=3 pump separation) [RELEVANT]
  - However: the period-m is set by pump configuration, not emerging from
    internal dynamics. The tanh^3 nonlinearity is NOT present — this uses
    Kerr (chi^3) cubic nonlinearity directly.
  - The FSR of 32.8 GHz does not map to any {2,3,5,31} signature.
  - VERDICT: Period-5 and period-3 subharmonics exist in optical DTC platforms
    but are engineered, not emergent from CUFT-RASP dynamics.

Paper 3: HIGHER-ORDER AND FRACTIONAL DTCs IN FLOQUET-DRIVEN RYDBERG ATOMS
Source: Ding et al. Nature Communications 15, 9730 (2024)
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53712-5
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13657

PLATFORM: Cesium Rydberg atoms (49P_{3/2} state)
  - Glass cell: 7 cm length, probe waist ~200 um
  - Electrode spacing: 4 cm
  - Three-photon EIT scheme for preparation/measurement

DRIVING PROTOCOL: Periodic pulsed RF field
  - RF frequency: omega = 2*pi * 7.1 MHz (standard), 2*pi * 5.8 MHz (higher-order)
  - RF field strength: E_RF = 0.30-0.75 V/cm
  - Driving frequencies: f_0 = 2*pi * {10, 40, 50, 60, 100, 150} kHz

INTEGER n-DTCs OBSERVED:
  - n = 2 (period-2)     [at f_0 = 2*pi * 10 kHz]
  - n = 3 (period-3)     [at f_0 = 2*pi * 10 kHz]    [RELEVANT]
  - n = 4 (period-4)     [at f_0 = 2*pi * 10 kHz]
  - n = 8, 9            [at f_0 = 2*pi * 100 kHz]
  - n = 14              [at f_0 = 2*pi * 150 kHz]

  >>> NOTE: PERIOD-5 DTC WAS NOT REPORTED IN THIS PAPER <<<

FRACTIONAL DTCs OBSERVED:
  - n = 11/2 (response at 0.182*f_0)
  - n = 125/29 (response at 0.232*f_0)
  - n = 100/29 (response at 0.29*f_0)

  The fraction 125/29 is noteworthy: 125 = 5^3 (=p^3 in CUFT-RASP).
  However, 29 is NOT in {2,3,5,31}. This is likely coincidence.

PHASE TRANSITION:
  - Critical detuning: Delta_c = -2*pi * 12.44 MHz (2-DTC to 3-DTC transition)
  - Interaction strength: V = -20.51*gamma (mean-field)
  - Critical exponent: lambda = 0.5

CUFT-RASP OVERLAP ANALYSIS:
  - Period-3 confirmed (n=3 in CUFT-RASP). HOWEVER: period-3 DTCs are
    common in many-body systems and not specific to (n,p)=(3,5).
  - Period-5 NOT observed, which would have been more distinctive.
  - The Rydberg platform uses spin-flip dynamics, not cubic-gate maps.
  - Fractional DTC 125/29: numerator = 5^3 is suggestive but denominator
    29 breaks {2,3,5,31} closure. Not a match.
  - VERDICT: No specific CUFT-RASP signature overlap. Period-3 exists
    but is generic to DTC physics, not specific to (3,5).

Paper 4: EXPERIMENTAL REALIZATION OF DISCRETE TIME QUASICRYSTALS
Source: He, Ye, Gong, Yao, Liu, Murch, Yao, Zu. Phys. Rev. X 15, 011055 (2025)
URL: https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011055
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17842

PLATFORM: NV center ensemble in diamond
  - NV density: ~4.5 ppm
  - Rabi frequency: Omega = (2*pi) * 8.3 MHz
  - Average dipolar coupling: J_bar ~ (2*pi) * 0.05 MHz
  - On-site random field: h_bar ~ (2*pi) * 1.7 MHz

DRIVING PROTOCOL: Quasiperiodic pulse sequences (NOT periodic)
  - Two incommensurate frequencies: omega_1, omega_2
  - Frequency ratio: omega_1/omega_2 = golden ratio phi = (sqrt(5)+1)/2 ~ 1.618
  - Short interaction times: tau_1 = 0.25 us, tau_2 = 0.404 us
  - Long interaction times: tau_1 = 2.00 us, tau_2 = 3.236 us
  - Rotation angles: (1-epsilon)*pi with |epsilon| << 1
  - Imperfection range: epsilon in [-0.07, 0.05]

SUBHARMONIC RESPONSES OBSERVED:
  - Z_2 phase: peaks at nu = (N_1 + 1/2)*omega_1 + (N_2 + 1/2)*omega_2
    with (N_1,N_2) = (0,0), (0,-1), (-1,1)
  - Z_2 x Z_2 phase: four dominant peaks at
    {(omega_1+omega_2)/2, (omega_1-omega_2)/2, (-omega_1+3*omega_2)/2, omega_1/2}

CUFT-RASP OVERLAP ANALYSIS:
  - Golden ratio phi = (1+sqrt(5))/2 contains sqrt(5). The number 5 = p
    in CUFT-RASP. However: phi is irrational and does not produce rational
    {2,3,5,31} signatures.
  - The quasiperiodic drive is fundamentally different from the CUFT-RASP
    periodic cubic-gate map.
  - No {2,3,5,31} frequency ratios observed.
  - VERDICT: No overlap. Different physics (quasiperiodicity vs periodic map).

  - No frequency ratios involving {2,3,5,31} reported.
  - VERDICT: Different class of time crystal. No overlap.

SECTION 4: GAP ANALYSIS — WHAT EXPERIMENT NEEDS TO BE RUN
========================================================================

NO existing experiment implements the CUFT-RASP cubic-gate map. All current
DTC experiments fall into two categories:

1. MANY-BODY SPIN SYSTEMS (Rydberg, NV centers, trapped ions, superconducting qubits)
   - Use collective spin dynamics, not single-variable iterative maps
   - Period-n arises from Floquet eigenvalue structure of many-body Hamiltonian
   - Not directly comparable to f(x) = 25*tanh^3(x) - x/124

2. OPTICAL/PHOTONIC SYSTEMS (Kerr cavities, transmission lines)
   - Use chi^3 Kerr nonlinearity (cubic in E-field)
   - Period-n set by pump configuration, not emergent from dynamics
   - Closest to CUFT-RASP in spirit (nonlinear dynamics) but wrong nonlinearity

THE EXPERIMENT THAT NEEDS TO BE RUN:

A. MICROWAVE CAVITY (lowest cost, fastest timeline):
   - Platform: 10 GHz microwave cavity with temporal modulation
   - Modulation profile: n(t) = n_0 + Delta_n * tanh^3(A*sin(2*pi*f_0*t))
     NOT sinusoidal modulation (which gives sin^3, not tanh^3)
   - Key observable: Fourier spectrum of output signal
   - Scan amplitude A from 0.5 to 5.0
   - Look for b_3/b_5 crossover near A ~ 2.0 (CUFT-RASP-SPECIFIC)
   - Look for frequency components at f_0/{2,3,5,31,124}
   - Cost estimate (from CUFT-RASP design file): $57-154K
   - Timeline: 3-6 months

B. ITO ENZ OPTICAL PLATFORM (higher precision):
   - Platform: ITO (indium tin oxide) at epsilon-near-zero frequency (~193.5 THz)
   - Ultrafast optical pump shapes tanh^3 temporal profile
   - Cost: $200-500K, timeline: 6-12 months

C. SILICON PHOTONIC PLATFORM (integrated, scalable):
   - Platform: silicon photonic circuit at ~193.5 THz
   - Ring resonator with shaped temporal modulation
   - Cost: $300-600K, timeline: 12-18 months

CRITICAL DISTINCTION: All three platforms require implementing the SPECIFIC
tanh^3 temporal modulation profile. No existing experiment uses this.
The closest existing work (Kerr cavity DTCs) uses instantaneous Kerr
nonlinearity, which is chi^3 * |E|^2 * E — cubic in field but NOT the
same as tanh^3 applied to a single variable iteratively.

SECTION 7: OPEN QUESTIONS
========================================================================

1. Can tanh^3 temporal modulation be synthesized in a microwave cavity using
   arbitrary waveform generators? The sigmoid saturation profile differs from
   Kerr instantaneous nonlinearity — is hardware shaping at GHz rates feasible?

2. The Rydberg DTC paper (Paper 3) observed n = {2,3,4,8,9,14} but skipped
   n = 5, 6, 7, 10-13. Was period-5 absent for physical reasons, or simply
   not in the parameter range explored? The detuning scan may not have
   covered the period-5 window.

3. The fractional DTC at 125/29: the numerator 125 = 5^3 = p^3 matches
   CUFT-RASP. Is 29 = 31-2 = Phi_3(5)-2 meaningful, or pure coincidence?
   No theoretical framework connects these.

4. Kerr cavity DTCs (Papers 1-2) use chi^3 nonlinearity, which produces
   E^3 coupling. CUFT-RASP uses tanh^3, which includes ALL odd powers
   (3rd + 5th + 7th + ...). Does the quintic correction from tanh^3
   expansion produce measurably different subharmonic spectra vs pure chi^3?

5. Could a mode-locked laser with appropriate saturable absorber profile
   naturally implement tanh^3 dynamics? Saturable absorption follows
   alpha(I) = alpha_0 / (1 + I/I_sat), which produces tanh-like profiles
   when integrated.

6. The coupled-lattice CUFT-RASP model predicts particle masses from
   coupled DTC orbits. Has any experiment coupled two independent DTC
   systems and measured the beat frequency spectra?

7. The gain-coherence condition |f'(x_u)|^3 ~ Gamma is within 0.93% for
   (3,5). Could this be measured in a nonlinear feedback circuit as a
   direct analog computer implementation, bypassing photonic platforms entirely?
