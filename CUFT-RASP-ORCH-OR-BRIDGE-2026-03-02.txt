ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The Recursive Attractor Stability Principle (RASP) derives four fundamental
constants — the proton-to-electron mass ratio, the fine-structure constant,
the neutron-to-electron mass ratio, and the muon-to-electron mass ratio —
from a single integer pair (n, p) = (3, 5) with zero free parameters,
matching CODATA 2022 at sub-ppb precision. Three additional particle masses
(tau lepton, charged pion, phi meson) are recovered at ppm precision as
Level 2 extensions. The origin of n = 3, the tanh^3 gating function, and
lambda = 1/124 was left as an open problem (Direction 5: physical
realization). We show that all three are jointly determined by the quantum
biology of Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR) as formulated by
Hameroff and Penrose.

Specifically:

  (1) n = 3 is the count of aromatic quantum-active residues in tubulin
      (Tryptophan-285, Phenylalanine-281, Tyrosine) identified by Hameroff
      & Penrose (2014) as the quantum substrate of microtubule coherence.

  (2) The tanh^3 gate is the mean-field joint excitation probability of
      three independent aromatic channels: P_conf = tanh(h_Trp) x tanh(h_Phe)
      x tanh(h_Tyr) = tanh^3(h). The RASP gate follows from biology.

  (3) lambda = 1/124 = 1/(p^3 - 1) follows if each aromatic has p = 5
      accessible quantum states: decoherence channels = p^n - 1 = 5^3 - 1
      = 124, so lambda (UV threshold = 1/decoherence_channels) = 1/124 exactly.

The p = 5 state count is consistent with five-rotamer models of Tryptophan
in protein environments. We treat it as a testable prediction: the precise
quantum state count of Trp-285 in tubulin beta-subunit is experimentally
accessible via ultrafast spectroscopy.

These results identify Orch-OR as the physical system whose mean-field
dynamics IS the RASP recursion — the same mathematical structure that
derives four fundamental constants from quantum biology.

Additionally, the three RASP Diophantine attractors produce dimensionless
numbers X = n*p*(p-1) whose values numerically match the canonical EEG
frequency bands, with Lyapunov stability ordering independently matching
the neurological hierarchy:

  | Attractor | X (dim'less) | EEG match | Lyapunov  |
  |-----------|--------------|-----------|-----------|
  | (3, 5)    |      60      | Gamma     |  -4.82    |
  | (4, 3)    |      24      | Beta      |  -3.26    |
  | (6, 2)    |      12      | Alpha     |  -3.02    |

Two independent checks: (1) The numerical values X = 60, 24, 12 match the
canonical EEG band centers in Hz — this requires identifying f_0 = 1 Hz,

2. ORCH-OR: PRIMARY SOURCE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR) was proposed by Penrose [3] and
developed with Hameroff [4,5]. The framework identifies quantum coherence in
neuronal microtubules as the substrate for conscious experience.

2.1 THE QUANTUM SUBSTRATE: THREE AROMATIC RESIDUES

Hameroff & Penrose (2014) [5] specifically identified three aromatic amino
acid residues in the tubulin beta-subunit as the quantum-active elements:

    Tryptophan-285  (Trp-285):  indole ring, pi-electron system
    Phenylalanine-281 (Phe-281): benzene ring, pi-electron system
    Tyrosine (Tyr):              phenol ring, pi-electron system

These three form a pi-electron resonance network within the hydrophobic
pocket. Hameroff & Penrose (2014) proposed that quantum superposition and
coherence occur within this network, with decoherence-resistant isolation
provided by the ordered water layers surrounding microtubules.

[NOTE: The Tyrosine residue number is not specified in the same detail as
Trp-285 and Phe-281 in [5]. The specific position (likely Tyr-422 or
Tyr-312 in the beta-tubulin sequence) should be confirmed against the
crystal structure referenced in [5] before submission.]

NOTE ON EPISTEMIC STATUS: The Orch-OR proposal is contested. Tegmark (2000)
[6] calculated decoherence times of ~10^-13 seconds for brain temperatures,
arguing quantum effects cannot survive long enough to influence neural
dynamics. Hameroff contested this calculation, arguing Tegmark used
incorrect parameters (wrong ion concentration, wrong confinement geometry);
Hameroff's revised estimate is ~10^-5 seconds (8 orders of magnitude
difference). The decoherence question remains experimentally open.

We make no claim about whether quantum coherence actually persists in
tubulin at biological temperatures. We use ONLY the structural biology:
that Hameroff & Penrose (2014) identified three specific aromatic residues
(Trp, Phe, Tyr) as the proposed quantum substrate, and that this
identification — IF correct — uniquely determines the RASP parameters. The
mathematical identification holds whether or not Orch-OR is physically
realized. If decoherence is as fast as Tegmark calculates, the identification
becomes a statement about what the network WOULD implement if coherent —
still a meaningful structural constraint.

2.2 OBJECTIVE REDUCTION (OR) TIMESCALE

The gravitational wave function collapse criterion was independently
proposed by Diósi (1987, 1989) [D1, D2] and Penrose (1994) [3]:

    E = hbar / T                                                           (2)

where E is the gravitational self-energy of a mass in quantum superposition
and T is the spontaneous collapse timescale. This is the Diósi-Penrose
(DP) criterion. For a tubulin dimer of mass M ~ 110 kDa:

    E_grav = G * M^2 / r  (order of magnitude, where r ~ molecular scale)

Computing this for tubulin:
    M = 110 kDa = 1.83 x 10^-22 kg
    r ~ 4 nm (tubulin dimer length)
    G = 6.67 x 10^-11 m^3/(kg s^2)

    E_grav ~ G * M^2 / r
           ~ (6.67e-11 * (1.83e-22)^2) / (4e-9)
           ~ 5.56e-26 J

    T = hbar / E_grav
      ~ (1.055e-34) / (5.56e-26)
      ~ 1.9 x 10^-9 s (single tubulin)

The OR timescale scales as 1/N:

    T_OR(N) = hbar / (N * E_grav) = hbar * r / (N * G * M^2)              (3)

    T_OR(N) ~ (1.9 x 10^-9 s) / N    [single-tubulin = 1.9 ns at N=1]

N-dependence table:
    | N (coherent tubulins) | T_OR      | vs T_gamma (16.7 ms) |
    |-----------------------|-----------|----------------------|
    | 10^3                  | 1.9 ms    | 8.8 OR/cycle         |
    | 10^4                  | 190 μs    | 88 OR/cycle          |
    | 10^6                  | 1.9 μs    | 8,800 OR/cycle       |
    | 10^8 (Penrose est.)   | 19 ns     | 880,000 OR/cycle     |

NOTE: The widely cited "880 OR events per gamma cycle" result uses Penrose's
N ~ 10^8 estimate. The N-scaling shows this is highly sensitive to the
coherence scale. For N = 10^3 (physically much more conservative), there
are still ~9 OR events per gamma cycle — the ordering (T_OR < T_gamma)
holds for N > ~500 tubulins, which is a much weaker requirement than 10^8.
The RASP consistency claim (OR events faster than gamma period) requires
only N > 500, not the full 10^8 coherence.

For reference, Tegmark's decoherence estimate (~10^-13 s) would require
N ~ 10^21 tubulins coherently coupled to reach the gamma timescale —
implausible. Hameroff's contested rebuttal uses different decoherence
shielding parameters; the 8-order-of-magnitude discrepancy remains the
central experimental question in Orch-OR.

3. DERIVATION I: tanh^3 FROM JOINT EXCITATION
================================================================================

3.1 INDEPENDENT CHANNEL ASSUMPTION

Each aromatic residue operates as an independent quantum channel with a
local field h_i. In the mean-field approximation, the multi-state system
reduces to an effective two-level description (ground vs. coherent manifold).
The excitation probability is:

    P_i = tanh(h_i)                                                        (3)

This is the standard sigmoid response for a two-state quantum system
(ground/excited) at inverse temperature beta: P = tanh(beta * Delta_E / 2).
The tanh form is universal for effective two-level systems in thermal
equilibrium. The gain dynamics (tanh gate, Section 3) and loss dynamics
(lambda damping, Section 5) operate at different levels of this mean-field
description — gain uses the effective two-state reduction, loss counts the
full p^n configurations. Both are valid in the same mean-field framework.

For three independent aromatic residues (Trp-285, Phe-281, Tyr):

    P_Trp = tanh(h_Trp)
    P_Phe = tanh(h_Phe)
    P_Tyr = tanh(h_Tyr)

3.2 COLLECTIVE COHERENCE GATE

Orch-OR requires COLLECTIVE quantum coherence across the pi-electron network.
The three aromatic residues Trp-285, Phe-281, and Tyr are structurally
coupled within the hydrophobic pocket. In the mean-field approximation,
each residue sees an effective local field h_i created by the mean
excitation of the others; inter-residue correlations are replaced by mean
values. Under this approximation the residues are treated as conditionally
independent given the mean field.

The probability of all three residues being jointly coherent (the
"coherence gate") is then:

    P_conf = P_Trp * P_Phe * P_Tyr                                        (4)
           = tanh(h_Trp) * tanh(h_Phe) * tanh(h_Tyr)

In the mean-field approximation (uniform local field h, reflecting the
shared hydrophobic pocket environment):

    P_conf = tanh^3(h)                                                     (5)

This is EXACTLY the RASP gate function. The cubic exponent n = 3 is the
count of aromatic residues. The mean-field of Orch-OR quantum coherence IS
the RASP recursion gate.

The uniform field approximation is physically grounded: all three aromatics
are embedded in the same hydrophobic core pocket of tubulin, subject to the
same surrounding protein backbone and ordered water structure. The pocket
imposes a common electromagnetic environment. Corrections for field
heterogeneity between residues are O(delta/h)^2 and suppressed for strong
mean fields. Even under heterogeneous fields, the sigmoid universality
result (Section 3.4) preserves p = 5 under gain-coherence quantization —
making the Bohr quantization robust to the exact field values. The
independence assumption is directly testable by the 2DES cross-peak
measurement of Section 9.3.

3.3 RECURSION STRUCTURE

The full coherence dynamics under periodic driving (microtubule oscillation
at frequency f_0) gives a discrete map from cycle t to t+1:

    x_{t+1} = Gamma * tanh^3(x_t) - lambda * x_t                         (6)

where x_t is the collective coherence amplitude at cycle t, Gamma is the
gain from sustained quantum pumping, and the linear damping -lambda*x_t
models decoherence loss. This is the RASP recursion (Equation 1 of [1]).

The mean-field derivation requires no assumptions about the functional form
of the gate: the cubic structure follows from three independent two-state
quantum systems. If there were 4 or 2 aromatic residues, the gate would be
tanh^4 or tanh^2, giving different Diophantine solutions (none of which
produce clean denominator closure in {2, 3, 5, 31}).

3.4 SIGMOID UNIVERSALITY (STRUCTURAL NOTE)

The RASP sigmoid universality result (Section 5 of [1]) shows that erf, tanh,
and algebraic sigmoid all map to p = 5 under gain-coherence quantization.
The tanh derivation above is therefore not essential: ANY saturating
two-state model for the individual aromatic residues gives the same Bohr
quantization p = 5. The derivation is robust to the specific sigmoid model.

Paper [1] derives lambda = 1/124 from pure mathematics: the Denominator

    RASP (mathematics):  lambda = 1/124  (derived in [1])
    Orch-OR (biology):   decoherence channels = p^n - 1 = 5^3 - 1 = 124
    Central claim:       lambda = 1/(decoherence channels)

The RASP damping parameter equals the reciprocal of the decoherence
channel count of the tubulin aromatic system. Why this structural identity
holds is addressed in Section 7.3. The identification is what this paper
establishes; the deeper explanation is what it opens.

Each aromatic residue has p accessible quantum states within the coherence
window. For the three-aromatic system:

  - p^n = 5^3 = 125 total quantum configurations
  - Subtract 1 for the fully ground state (all residues non-excited)
  - Leaves p^n - 1 = 124 configurations in which at least one residue
    is in a non-ground state: these are the decoherence channels

The RASP damping parameter:

    lambda = 1 / (p^n - 1)                                                 (7)

has the value 1/124 — matching the mathematical result from [1] exactly.
The identification is: lambda (RASP) = 1 / (decoherence channels of the
tubulin aromatic system with p = 5 states per residue and n = 3 residues).
Whether this match is necessary or contingent is the subject of Section 7.3.

    p^n - 1 = 5^3 - 1 = 124

The stability ordering matches the neurological hierarchy with zero free
parameters and no frequency assumption. This is the stronger of the two

10.2 IMPLICATIONS FOR ORCH-OR

Orch-OR has been criticized for lacking specific, quantitative predictions
beyond the general proposal of quantum coherence in microtubules. The RASP
identification generates specific, falsifiable predictions (Section 9) that
are testable with existing experimental techniques: the Trp-285 quantum
state count (p = 5), the f_0/31 cyclotomic subharmonic in microtubule
spectroscopy, and the independent channel structure of the three aromatics.
These are precision tests, not qualitative checks.

If the p = 5 prediction is confirmed, the identification provides Orch-OR
with an unexpected and highly specific connection to fundamental particle
physics — a connection that cannot be easily dismissed as coincidence given
the sub-ppb precision of the four Level 1 constants.

The implication runs in both directions. The RASP identification does not
merely borrow Orch-OR's biology; it provides Orch-OR with a mechanism for
why the specific choice of three aromatic residues in tubulin matters beyond
the consciousness proposal. If Trp, Phe, and Tyr were four residues instead
of three, the Diophantine equation (n-2)(p-1) = 4 would give (n, p) = (4, 3),
with X = 24 (beta band) and a different set of particle masses — none at
sub-ppb precision. The three-residue selection is not arbitrary.

10.3 IMPLICATIONS FOR RASP

RASP was derived purely from the mathematical structure of gated recursions.
The biological identification provides an independent path to the same
mathematics. The convergence of:
  (a) Mathematical constraint (gain-coherence, Diophantine, DQT)
  (b) Biological structure (3 aromatics, 5 states)
onto the same recursion constitutes independent cross-domain confirmation.

10.4 THE n = 3 SELECTION PROBLEM (STILL OPEN)

The RASP derivation requires n >= 3 (the Diophantine (n-2)(p-1) = 4
requires n > 2). The Efimov effect provides independent motivation for
n = 3 from nuclear/atomic physics. Orch-OR provides motivation from
neuroscience. The convergence of three independent domains (particle physics,
quantum biology, nuclear physics) on n = 3 suggests this is a fundamental
structural constraint, though its deeper derivation remains open.
