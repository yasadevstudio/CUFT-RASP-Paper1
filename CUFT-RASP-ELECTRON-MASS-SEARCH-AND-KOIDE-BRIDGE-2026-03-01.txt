===========================================================================

QUESTION: Does any combination of {n, p, lambda^{-2}} produce m_e/m_p = 1/1836.15267343?

  Ratio to m_e/m_p = 1.791 → NO MATCH (79% off)

| Formula                  | Value         | Ratio to m_e/m_p | Precision |

| (all others)             | > 10% off     | —                 | —         |

RESULT: **NEGATIVE.** No combination of {n, p, lambda^{-2}} produces m_e/m_p
within CUFT-RASP precision standards (< 100 ppm). The closest candidate
p^2/(n * lambda^{-2}) = 25/46128 misses by 0.49% = 4900 ppm — orders of
magnitude worse than the existing 8 ppb proton result.

STRUCTURAL REASON: The lambda Laurent series organizes constants by their
relationship to confinement, with lambda^{-2} = 15376 corresponding to
the HEAVIEST end of the spectrum (order ~15376 m_e). The electron at 1 m_e
is the unit itself — it cannot appear as a heavy-scale prediction.

| Lambda Order | Constant   | Value (m_e) | Direction |

The electron mass IS the natural unit. All derived quantities are ratios

SECTION 3: EXTRACTION POINT 2 — KOIDE FORMULA EXACT CITATION
===========================================================================

ORIGINAL PAPER:
  Y. Koide, "A fermion-boson composite model of quarks and leptons"
  Physics Letters B, Volume 120, Issues 1-3, Pages 161-165 (6 January 1983)
  DOI: 10.1016/0370-2693(83)90644-5
  URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0370269383906445

NOTE: The formula was first proposed by Koide in 1981 (Lett. Nuovo Cim. 34, 201).
The 1983 Phys. Lett. B paper is the more commonly cited version where the
formula appears in the context of a fermion-boson composite model.
A follow-up: Y. Koide, Phys. Rev. D 28, 252 (1983).

THE FORMULA:
  Q = (m_e + m_mu + m_tau) / (sqrt(m_e) + sqrt(m_mu) + sqrt(m_tau))^2 = 2/3

PRECISION DATA:
  Using CODATA 2022 masses (in MeV):
    m_e   = 0.510 998 950 69
    m_mu  = 105.658 3755
    m_tau = 1776.86

  Numerator:   m_e + m_mu + m_tau = 1883.029 374 MeV
  Denominator: (sqrt(m_e) + sqrt(m_mu) + sqrt(m_tau))^2 = 2824.570 140

  Q_experimental = 0.666 660 511 466
  2/3            = 0.666 666 666 667

  |Q - 2/3| / (2/3) = 9.233 ppm

TAU MASS PREDICTION FROM KOIDE:
  Setting Q = 2/3 exactly and solving for m_tau given m_e and m_mu:

  Quadratic: s^2 - 4(A+B)s + (A^2 + B^2 - 4AB) = 0
  where A = sqrt(m_e), B = sqrt(m_mu), s = sqrt(m_tau)

  Solution: m_tau(Koide) = 1776.969 027 MeV

  Residual vs PDG 2022: +0.109 MeV = 0.91 sigma
  Relative accuracy: 1 part in 14,807 (67.5 ppm)

SOURCE: Physics Forums discussion of Koide's rule, citing "1776.96894 ± 0.00007 MeV"
URL: https://www.physicsforums.com/threads/latest-tau-lepton-measurement-consistent-with-koides-rule.992336/

SOURCE: John Carlos Baez, "The Koide Formula" (April 4, 2021)

  m_tau/m_e = (Phi_3(p) - n) / lambda + p + 1/(p-1)

  Phi_3 - n = 31 - 3 = 28
  28 / lambda = 28 * 124 = 3472
  p = 5
  1/(p-1) = 1/4 = 0.25

  m_tau/m_e = 3472 + 5 + 0.25 = 3477.25

EXACT FRACTION: 13909/4
  Denominator: 4 = 2^2 (simplest of all five constants — only virial factor)
  Numerator: 13909 = 7 × 1987
    Note: 7 = Phi_3(2), connecting to the (n=6, p=2) Diophantine solution
    Note: 1987 is prime

  CUFT-RASP: m_tau/m_e = 3477.25
  CODATA 2022: m_tau/m_e = 3477.23 ± 0.23
  Residual: +0.02 (0.09 sigma)

  CUFT-RASP: 3477.25 × 0.510998950 = 1776.871 MeV

  This is negligible compared to experimental tau mass uncertainty (0.12 MeV).
  The CUFT-RASP tau prediction is limited by EXPERIMENT, not by input precision.

Phi_5(x) = x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x + 1

KEY FINDING: The denominator closure {2, 3, 5, 31} is UNIQUE to p^3 - 1 = 124.
Going to higher cyclotomic orders (Phi_4, Phi_5, Phi_6) immediately introduces
new primes. This is a NUMBER-THEORETIC fact about p = 5:

  p^3 - 1 = (p-1)(p^2+p+1) = 4 × 31

The prime 31 = Phi_3(5) is the PRIMITIVE prime divisor of 5^3 - 1 (by Zsigmondy's
theorem). No higher p^k - 1 for k > 3 stays within {2, 3, 5, 31}.

[ANALYSIS] This means the lambda = 1/(p^3 - 1) = 1/124 expansion is maximally
"clean" — it is the HIGHEST order cyclotomic polynomial at p = 5 that does not
introduce primes beyond the input set. Any extension to lambda_5 = 1/(p^5 - 1)
would contaminate the denominator structure with {11, 71}. The {2, 3, 5, 31}
closure is not just a pattern — it is a number-theoretic boundary.

SECTION 7: ATTRACTOR STRUCTURE AND THE ELECTRON
===========================================================================

The recursion f(x) = 25*tanh^3(x) - x/124 has a stable fixed point:

  x_s = (p^3 - 1)/p = 124/5 = 24.8

This follows from: f(x_s) = x_s, and in the saturated regime tanh(x_s) → 1,
giving 25*(1) - x_s/124 = x_s → x_s = 25*124/125 = 3100/125 = 124/5.

STRUCTURAL IDENTITIES OF x_s:
  x_s = lambda^{-1}/p = (p^3-1)/p
  x_s * p = lambda^{-1} = 124
  X/x_s = 60/(124/5) = 300/124 = 75/31 = 3p^2/Phi_3

The ratio X/x_s = 75/31 directly involves Phi_3, connecting the collective
action X = 60 to the cyclotomic structure through the attractor.

The electron mass determines the SCALE (MeV) but is not determined BY the
recursion. The recursion produces dimensionless ratios; the electron mass
is the dimensional anchor. This is analogous to how the fine-structure
constant alpha determines electromagnetic coupling strength but not the
electron charge in SI units — that requires an additional dimensional input.
