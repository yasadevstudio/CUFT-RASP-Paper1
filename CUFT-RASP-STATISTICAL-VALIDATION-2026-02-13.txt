OVERALL STATISTICAL ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

STRENGTHS:
  1. Primary prediction matches to 8 ppb — probability ~10^-5 by chance
  2. Information compression ratio 2.5-10.4:1 — genuine predictive content
  3. n=3 uniqueness theorem — non-trivial structural match to physics
  4. Sigmoid universality — robust to functional form choice
  5. Derivation chain is algebraically transparent and verifiable
  6. Residual source identified (lambda quantization) — not mysterious

WEAKNESSES:
  1. Framework predicts ONLY mu (one number), not a spectrum
  2. c_1 = n/p is Occam selection, not derivation (1 hidden free parameter)
  3. lambda ≈ 1.1 * alpha — no explanation
  4. Diophantine solutions (n=4,6) predict non-existent particles
  5. 460.8 sigma deviation from CODATA (using full precision uncertainty)
     — publication draft incorrectly states 134 sigma
  6. No predictions for alpha, G, or other fundamental constants
  7. Cornell potential mapping is qualitative (dimensionless vs physical units)

HONEST ASSESSMENT:
  CUFT-RASP is a remarkable mathematical observation that the proton-to-
  electron mass ratio can be approximated to 8 ppb by a simple recursion
  with n=3 quarks as the sole input. The information-theoretic compression
  is genuine. The structural theorems (n=3 uniqueness, sigmoid universality)
  are non-trivial mathematical results.

  However, calling this a "derivation" requires accepting the Occam
  completion as equivalent to formal derivation — which it is not.
  With c_1 as a free parameter, RASP has 1 input (n=3) and 1 adjustable
  coefficient, making the match less surprising (though still impressive).

  The framework's scope is extremely narrow: it produces ONE number (mu)
  and cannot predict any other fundamental constant. A genuine theory of
  fundamental physics would need to predict alpha, quark masses, coupling
  constants, and the particle spectrum. RASP does none of this.

  OVERALL GRADE: B+
  (Strong mathematical observation with genuine predictive content,
   but limited scope and one unresolved gap)
