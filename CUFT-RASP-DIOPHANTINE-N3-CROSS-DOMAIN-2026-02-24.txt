## 3. PHYSICS DERIVATIONS OF N=3 FROM DIOPHANTINE/INTEGER CONSTRAINTS

### 3a. The 331 Model (Pisano-Pleitez-Frampton, 1992)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Gauge group | SU(3)_C × SU(3)_L × U(1)_X |
| Original papers | Pisano & Pleitez (1992), Frampton (1992) |
| Key mechanism | Anomaly cancellation requires inter-family sum, not family-by-family |
| Result | N_gen = 3 is the UNIQUE solution for anomaly freedom |

**The mathematical mechanism:**

In the Standard Model, each family independently cancels gauge anomalies:
- SU(3)_C² U(1)_Y: cancels per family
- SU(2)_L² U(1)_Y: cancels per family
- [U(1)_Y]³: cancels per family
- [gravity]² U(1)_Y: cancels per family

In the 331 model, the SU(3)_L² U(1)_X anomaly does NOT cancel per family. The anomaly cancellation equation is:

    Sum_{i=1}^{N_gen} X_Qi = 0

where X_Qi are the U(1)_X charges of the left-handed quark triplets. Because one family transforms as a triplet and the other two as antitriplets under SU(3)_L, this sum vanishes ONLY when N_gen is a multiple of 3 (the number of colors N_c).

Combined with the QCD asymptotic freedom constraint (beta function < 0 requires N_flavors ≤ 16 for SU(3)_C), the unique solution is N_gen = 3.

Source: Montero, Pisano, Pleitez (1990s); confirmed by review: arXiv:2404.05821 (2024); PMC: PMC11119231.

Each intermediate radical pair is too short-lived for functional singlet-triplet interconversion. Only the TERMINAL pair (after all 3 transfers) has the correct distance and lifetime.

2. **Does the 331-type mechanism have a CUFT-RASP analog?** In the 331 model, n=3 emerges from requiring anomaly cancellation across families rather than within families. Is there an analogous "cross-component consistency" requirement in CUFT-RASP?
