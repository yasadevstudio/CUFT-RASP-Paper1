SECTION-BY-SECTION BREAKDOWN
-----------------------------

1. THE CORE EQUATION (Eq. 1) — The Heart of the Problem

   Ψ_coh,n+1 = F(Ψ_coh,n) + Σ β_nm Ψ_coh(x_m, t_m) e^(iϕ_nm)

   F() is never defined. This equation is just a generic discrete-time recurrence relation with phase
   factors. You could write this about literally any dynamical system. It's like saying "the next state
   equals some function of the current state plus some weighted sum of other states with phases."
   That's every dynamical system ever.

   Compare to what a real theory looks like:
   - Schrodinger: iℏ ∂Ψ/∂t = HΨ — H is explicitly defined for every system
   - Einstein: G_μν = 8πG/c⁴ T_μν — every tensor is calculable
   - Yang-Mills: Explicit gauge field Lagrangian with coupling constants

   CUFT's fundamental equation is a template, not a theory.

2. THE LAGRANGIAN (Eq. 5-6) — Textbook Plagiarism

   L = ½(∇Ψ_coh)² - V(Ψ_coh)
   V = ¼λ_coh Ψ⁴ - ½μ_coh Ψ²

   This is literally scalar φ⁴ theory with spontaneous symmetry breaking. This is the Mexican hat
   potential. Every physics undergrad sees this in their third year. It's the Higgs mechanism stripped
   of its gauge structure.

   Fatal problem: φ⁴ theory in 4D is trivial (the Landau pole / triviality problem). Known since the
   1980s. If CUFT rests on φ⁴, it inherits all of φ⁴'s problems — the EXACT problems the author
   claims to solve.

3. THE PROPAGATOR (Eq. 12) — This Is Not a Propagator

   G_coh(x) = Σ_n e^(-inω_coh t) F(Ψ_coh,n)

   A real propagator tells you: given a source at point A, what's the field at point B? It encodes
   causality, particle content (poles = particles), and interaction strength.

   CUFT's "propagator" is a Fourier series with F() still undefined. It doesn't have poles (no particle
   spectrum). Doesn't encode causality. Can't compute a cross-section from it. It's a Fourier series
   with a label.

4. CHARGE QUANTIZATION (Eq. 13-15) — Circular Reasoning

   Q_coh,n = n·e_coh
   e_coh ≈ e · (Ψ_coh / Ψ_base)

   This says: charge is quantized in units of e_coh, and e_coh equals the observed charge times a
   ratio. This is not a derivation — it's a definition. They're saying "charge equals charge times a
   fudge factor."

   Real charge quantization explanations:
   - Dirac (1931): If magnetic monopoles exist, e·g = nℏc/2, forcing quantization from topology
   - Standard Model: SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) anomaly cancellation constrains charge ratios
   - Grand Unification: SU(5) or SO(10) embedding forces e/3 quark charges

   CUFT never shows HOW phase-locking produces e = 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C specifically.

5. EMERGENT GRAVITY (Eq. 16) — Dimensionally Suspicious

   G_coh(x,t) = -∇Ψ_coh · ∇Ψ_coh

   Problems:
   - Always negative: -|∇Ψ|² ≤ 0 always. Real gravity can be attractive or repulsive.
   - Scalar, not tensor: Real gravity is rank-2 tensor (g_μν). Can't describe frame-dragging,
     gravitational waves with 2 polarizations, etc.
   - No 1/r² recovery: For point source Ψ ~ 1/r, then ∇Ψ ~ 1/r², so G_coh ~ 1/r⁴.
     That's NOT Newton's gravity. This is the single biggest mathematical failure in the paper.
   - No Einstein limit: No metric, no geodesic equation, no equivalence principle.

   α_coh = f(Ψ_coh, phase locking)    [Eq. 18]
   α_coh ∝ Ψ_coh^γ                    [Eq. 19]

   sentence disguised as math. γ is defined circularly using α₀ (the thing you're trying to derive).

   QED derives α from first principles and matches experiment to 10 decimal places (g-2 of electron).
